# Property Liquidity

## 1. Segments & Problems

**Investment property owners:** Want to sell at an attractive return but don't know how quickly they can sell their property.

**Buyers:** Are afraid of buying a property they won't be able to sell later.

## 2. Why Property Finder?

PF has a unique combination of data to assess property liquidity:

- **PropWorth:** Property valuations, estimated price ranges and information about properties owners monitor.
- **Listings:** Asking prices, competing inventory, property characteristics and listing history.
- **Buyer behaviour:** Searches, saves and enquiries reveal demand for specific areas and property types.
- **DLD transactions:** Actual sale prices, transaction volumes and historical market activity.

Together, these sources allow PF to assess liquidity through buyer demand, available supply and completed sales, both for individual properties and market segments.

## 3. Solution

**Owners:** Property Liquidity Score, buyer demand, competing supply and comparable sales. Monthly updates help owners monitor selling conditions and decide when to approach an agent.

**Buyers:** Compare liquidity across areas, property types and bedrooms. Evaluate individual properties before buying and continue monitoring after purchase.

**Journey:** Explore → Buy → Monitor → Sell.

## 4. Business Impact

| Metric | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Owner retention | Monthly liquidity updates |
| Seller leads | Agent introductions |
| Buyer enquiries | Better-informed purchase decisions |
| Listing supply | More owners entering the selling journey |

## 5. Data Strategy

**Opportunities:** Personalised owner updates, buyer liquidity insights, consent-based seller leads and aggregated market intelligence for agents and developers.

**Infrastructure:** Match PropWorth, PF listings and DLD data; maintain monthly snapshots; validate liquidity scores against actual transactions.

**Privacy:** Never disclose private ownership information or inferred selling intentions without consent.

**Prototype data:** DLD transactions and historical Bayut listings; simulated buyer behaviour and Liquidity Scores.

## 6. Success Test

**Primary KPI:** Meaningful monthly returning owners.

A/B test on 1% of eligible PropWorth owners: valuation only vs valuation with liquidity monitoring.

**Target:** +20% relative monthly retention. Track seller leads as a secondary metric and notification opt-outs as a guardrail. Confirm statistical power before launch.
